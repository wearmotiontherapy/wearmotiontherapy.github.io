We started Motion Therapy as a group of friends who noticed the same thing: the days we moved were the days our heads felt clear. A run before school, a lift after a bad test, a long walk when everything felt like too much.

So we made clothes for that. Pieces you can train in, recover in, and wear everywhere after. A lifestyle brand for a stronger tomorrow.
